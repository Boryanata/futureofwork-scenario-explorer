# Data Sources

This document will track source URLs, download dates, file names, and usage notes.

## O*NET

Source: https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html

Downloaded files should be placed in `data/raw/onet/`.

## BLS OEWS

Source: https://www.bls.gov/oes/tables.htm

Downloaded files should be placed in `data/raw/bls/`.

## OECD

Downloaded files should be placed in `data/raw/oecd/`.

## AIOE

Source: https://github.com/AIOE-Data/AIOE

Downloaded files should be placed in `data/raw/aioe/`.

Citation:

Felten, E., Raj, M., & Seamans, R. (2021). Occupational, industry, and geographic exposure to artificial intelligence: A novel dataset and its potential uses. Strategic Management Journal, 42(12), 2195-2217.

## Anthropic Economic Index

Source: https://www.anthropic.com/economic-index

Downloaded files should be placed in `data/raw/anthropic/`.

## ILOSTAT Industrial Relations Data

Source: https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/

Industrial relations definitions: https://ilostat.ilo.org/methods/concepts-and-definitions/description-industrial-relations-data/

Downloaded trade union density and collective bargaining coverage files should be placed in `data/raw/ilo/irdata/`.

## Policy Documents

Country-specific workforce, reskilling, AI governance, and labor-market policy documents should be placed in `data/raw/policy/`.
